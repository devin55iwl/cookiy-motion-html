Design Reflection

During building Cookiy, design decisions were never just visual. Each of these three challenges could fill an hour on its own.

Does Every AI Startup Eventually Lose Its App?

As intelligence becomes the product itself, interfaces begin to matter less. When users (AI-native) can simply describe outcomes, what role should software still play?

Confidentiality vs. Visibility?

Enterprise customers demand confidentiality. Startups depend on visibility. Designing between trust and exposure became one of the hardest parts of building a B2B product.

Enterprise Product, Consumer Dynamics?

Skill created a direct relationship between an enterprise platform and individual users. It challenged many assumptions I had about growth, adoption, and monetization.
