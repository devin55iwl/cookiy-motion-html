01 Context

Cookiy helps research teams move from discussion guide creation to participant recruitment, AI-moderated interviews, observation, insight generation, and reporting. The core problem was not whether AI could interview people, but whether researchers could trust the process.

03 Core Challenge

AI interviews cannot feel like automation running in the dark.

Researchers need to know what the AI is doing, how the session quality is changing, and when human intervention is needed.

04 Design System of Trust

Observe Room was not just a screen.

Observe Room was designed as a trust layer between AI interviews and human researchers. Instead of hiding automation behind a clean dashboard, it exposes the signals researchers need to evaluate, intervene, and make decisions.

01

Live Transcript

Researchers can follow the conversation as it happens, not after the AI has already summarized it.

02

AI Behavior Monitoring

The interface makes the AI moderator's current action, question path, and follow-up intent visible.

03

Participant Status

Status, recording, camera state, and session progress help researchers judge the reliability of the moment.

04

Quality Judgment

Signals such as sentiment, keywords, and quality scores turn raw conversation into research confidence.

05

Human Intervention

Researchers can take action when the AI needs help, rather than treating automation as untouchable.

06

Follow-up / Signals

Follow-up suggestions and emerging signals help teams decide what to ask next and what to verify later.

Design Reflection

The hardest product decisions sat between interface, trust, and go-to-market reality. These notes are the conversation after the case study, not the headline.

Does Every AI Startup Eventually Lose Its App?

As intelligence becomes the product itself, interfaces begin to matter less.

When AI-native users can simply describe outcomes, what role should software still play?

Enterprise Product, Consumer Dynamics?

The Skill layer created a direct relationship between an enterprise platform and individual users.

It challenged many assumptions I had about growth, adoption, and monetization.

Confidentiality vs. Visibility?

Enterprise customers demand confidentiality. Startups depend on visibility.

Designing between trust and exposure became one of the hardest parts of building a B2B product.
