Design System of Trust

Observe Room was never just a screen.

I designed it as a trust layer — rather than hiding the automation, it surfaces exactly what researchers need to evaluate quality, decide to intervene, and feel confident acting on the output.

The Decision · Signal surface

The debate

Show a single quality score — clean, fast, scannable. Or expose the raw signals behind it — sentiment, keywords, transcript — and let researchers form their own judgment.

Why I chose raw

A single number would feel authoritative — but if a researcher couldn't explain why quality was 71, they couldn't defend their findings. Trust requires legibility, not just confidence.

What I'd do differently

If I rebuilt Observe Room today, I'd reduce the signal surface earlier. We exposed everything we could — but we over‑indexed on visibility before we understood what researchers actually acted on. More signals isn't always more trust.

Does every AI startup eventually lose its app?

As intelligence becomes the product, the interface starts to matter less. When users can simply describe outcomes, what role does software still play?

Confidentiality vs. visibility?

Enterprise customers demand confidentiality. Startups depend on visibility. Designing between trust and exposure was one of the hardest problems on this product.
